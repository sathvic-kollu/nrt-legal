Privacy policy

NRT – Non Resident Tirupatian app (package app.tirupati.nrt) · Effective 17 November 2026 · Updated 25 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the NRT app collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and the choices you have. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store policies. By registering you consent to the processing described here; you can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

1. Data we collect

DataWhy
Google account email and Google user id (from Google Sign-In)To create and secure your account. Never shown to other members.
Display name, home area in Tirupati, current city and country, mobile numberRequired at registration. Name, home area and city appear on leaderboards and in the community. Your mobile number is never shown to other members, except when you choose to share it as a blood donor (section 4).

NRT has no advertising SDK, does not use your advertising ID and has no in-app purchases. Sponsor banners and sponsored posts are approved by NRT's administrators, labelled as sponsored and the same for every member; sponsors receive no personal data.

We do not collect your contacts, precise location, SMS or call logs.

2. Who processes your data

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and photo storage; servers in Mumbai, India)
  • Google: Sign-In and Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications), under Google's privacy policy

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with advertising networks. We disclose it only to these processors to run the app, or where Indian law requires.

3. What other members can see

Your display name, photo, home area, city and country, quiz points and badges, and content you post under your name. Phone number, email and date of birth are visible only to you and to the app's moderators and administrators when they need them to handle abuse or support requests.

4. Blood donor contact sharing

If you register as a donor and tap "I can help" on a blood request, the app shows a confirmation and, only after you agree, shares your mobile number with that one requester. Each share is logged. You can withdraw as a donor at any time, which deletes your donor details.

5. How long we keep data

  • Your account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Delivered notification records: 30 days. Alerts, greetings, contests and other posts: as long as they are shown, then deleted with your account.
  • Moderation and audit records: only as long as needed to handle repeat abuse and legal requests; reviewed at least once a year.

6. Your rights and choices

Under the DPDP Act you may access, correct and erase your personal data, withdraw consent, nominate a person to exercise these rights, and raise a grievance.

  • Correct your profile in the app (Profile → Edit).
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account). This permanently removes your profile, answers, messages and other content linked to you.
  • Notification choices can be changed in the app settings.
  • For anything else, or to raise a grievance, email our Grievance Officer at contact@nrt.com. We reply within 7 days and resolve within 30 days. If unresolved, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Children

NRT is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Anonymous rooms and some features are for adults (18+) only.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted by row-level security so members can read only what this policy allows, and every write is checked on the server.

9. Changes

We will post updates on this page and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
