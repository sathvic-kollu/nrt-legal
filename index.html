NRT – Non Resident Tirupatian

The community app for Tirupatians living in other cities and countries.

NRT brings the @non_resident_tirupatian community into one app:

  • A daily Tirupati quiz with weekly, monthly and city leaderboards
  • Tirupati updates: darshan and seva notices, events, weather, traffic and civic news
  • Moderated city and topic chat rooms, including anonymous rooms for adults
  • Alerts, greetings, NRT Heroes, weekend contests, city meetups, jobs and referrals, a help desk for families back home, a business directory and an opt-in blood donor list

Quizzes and contests are always free to enter. NRT is an independent community app and is not affiliated with Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD) or any government body.
